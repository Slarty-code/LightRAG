# TD 2024/1 (Synthetic) — General deductions and s 8-1

This synthetic Taxation Determination / practical-guidance stub is for LightRAG evaluation only. It is not an ATO instrument.

## Ruling summary

1. A loss or outgoing is deductible under **s 8-1** of the *Income Tax Assessment Act 1997* (Synthetic Mini) to the extent that it is incurred in gaining or producing assessable income, subject to the negative limbs in that section.
2. Apportionment is required where an outgoing has both deductible and non-deductible purposes. The phrase **to the extent** in s 8-1 is the operative apportionment language.
3. This determination does not alter the penalty rules in Part 4 of the synthetic Act. Administrative warnings about false statements are not a substitute for the Deduction Division tests.

## Example

A contractor buys a laptop used 70% for client work that produces assessable income and 30% for private use. Under s 8-1, only 70% of the outgoing is deductible to the extent of the income-producing use, assuming no capital prohibition applies.
