# PR 2024/1 (Synthetic) — Product ruling: general deductions

This synthetic Product Ruling stub is for LightRAG evaluation only. It is not an ATO instrument.

## Arrangement

This ruling considers whether participants in a synthetic income-producing arrangement can deduct outgoings under **s 8-1** of the *Income Tax Assessment Act 1997* (Synthetic Mini).

## Ruling

1. Outgoings incurred under the described arrangement are deductible under s 8-1 to the extent they are incurred in gaining or producing assessable income, subject to the negative limbs.
2. Where an outgoing has private and income-producing components, **apportionment** is required; see TD 2024/1 (Synthetic).
3. This ruling does not authorise deductions that would be denied as capital, private or domestic, or that would double-count a specific deduction under **s 8-5**.
