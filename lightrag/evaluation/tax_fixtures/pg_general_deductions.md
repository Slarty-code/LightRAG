# PG 2024/1 (Synthetic) — Practice guide: general deductions

This synthetic Practice Guide stub is for LightRAG evaluation only. It is not an ATO instrument.

## Scope

This guide explains how taxpayers apply the general deduction in **s 8-1** of the *Income Tax Assessment Act 1997* (Synthetic Mini). It summarises positive and negative limbs and points to apportionment via **to the extent**.

## Key points

1. Start with Division 8 — General deductions before considering administrative notes.
2. Apportion mixed-purpose outgoings under s 8-1; see also TD 2024/1 (Synthetic) for the operative reading of **to the extent**.
3. Specific deductions under **s 8-5** may apply even when s 8-1 would not; do not claim a double deduction.
